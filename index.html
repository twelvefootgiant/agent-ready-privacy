Agent Ready

Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Agent Ready is a Chrome extension that turns the web page you're reading into a markdown file. It runs entirely in your browser.

What it does with the pages you capture

When you click the Agent Ready toolbar button, it reads the page in the current tab, converts the article to markdown, and saves the result to your computer: your Downloads folder and, if you turn it on, your clipboard. It only reads a page when you click the button.

What it stores

  • Capture history. If history is on (the default), a copy of each capture, with its title, source URL, and date, is kept in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. You can delete any or all of it from the history page, turn history off in the options, and uninstalling the extension removes it.
  • Settings. Your choices (download folder, filename pattern, and so on) are saved with Chrome's sync storage, which Chrome may sync between your own signed-in browsers.

What it doesn't do

Agent Ready does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or browsing data. It makes no network requests, has no accounts, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking. Nothing you capture leaves your computer unless you share it yourself.
